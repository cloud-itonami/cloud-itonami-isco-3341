# Business Model: Independent Office Supervision Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-3341`
- ISCO-08: `3341`
- Occupation: Office Supervisors
- Social impact: workplace-coordination, administrative-efficiency, local-jobs

## Customer

- small and mid-sized offices
- clerical/administrative service providers

## Offer

- task-record and workflow logging
- clerical-staff shift/task scheduling
- office-supply coordination
- HR-concern surfacing for human review

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-workflow coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no proposal may finalize a disciplinary action, termination or
  performance-review determination — those decisions stay with a human,
  permanently
- every workflow/staff-member basis is independently verified against the
  registered roster and workflow board, not asserted by the advisor
- a supply order above the workflow's registered cost ceiling always
  requires human sign-off
- an HR concern is always surfaced for human review, never auto-resolved
- coordination and scheduling records are auditable, not editable
